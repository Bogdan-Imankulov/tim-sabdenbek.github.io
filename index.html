New Chapter: Canada

Starting from scratch — overnight shifts to support myself, training every day to stay at the highest level.

Through perseverance and hard work, I continued competing. During one of the competitions I was offered the opportunity to represent the United States. It marked a turning point in my career and opened the door to a new chapter on my journey toward the Olympic Games.
